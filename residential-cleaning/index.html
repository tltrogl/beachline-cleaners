What’s included

Your cleaning plan may include:

  • Kitchen surfaces, sink, appliance exteriors, and floors
  • Bathroom fixtures, mirrors, counters, and floors
  • Dusting reachable surfaces throughout the home
  • Vacuuming and mopping appropriate floor surfaces
  • Trash removal and basic room reset
  • Customized priorities when agreed in advance

Who this is for

A good fit for homes that need recurring maintenance or a one-time reset. Weekly, biweekly, and one-time service can be quoted based on the home and requested scope.

Options & Add-ons

  • + Inside refrigerator and oven
  • + Inside cabinets (if emptied)
  • + Interior windows

How pricing is determined

Pricing is determined by the size of the home, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and the frequency of service. Homes that have not been professionally cleaned recently may need an initial deep clean before moving to recurring maintenance service.

Service FAQs

Do I need to be home?

Let us know whether you plan to be home. If you will be out, agree on secure property access before the appointment.

Do you bring supplies?

We bring standard cleaning supplies and equipment for routine service. If your home has delicate surfaces, product preferences, or sensitivities, tell us before the visit.

What if I have pets?

Pets are welcome. Please secure any pet that is aggressive, highly anxious, or likely to interfere with cleaning so the visit can be completed safely.
