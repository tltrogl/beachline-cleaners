## Concept and customers

**Coastal Chaos Cleaning — From Chaos to Clean.**

Proposed model: recurring residential cleaning plus short-term-rental turnovers, with deep and move-in/move-out cleans filling gaps. Position around reliability, responsiveness, and clear communication. The supplied brief advises against launching as a generic low-price cleaner or leading with commercial janitorial work.

Proposed residential promise: “Reliable cleaning. Clear communication. No wondering whether your cleaner is actually showing up.”

Target vacation-rental customers: independent hosts with 1–5 properties and smaller property managers. The supplied research says some large managers have their own cleaning/laundry operations and cites one reporting 400+ properties. That does not establish demand for this company's services.

## Illustrative economics

Target realized revenue: $45–$55 per cleaning labor-hour. Two workers cleaning for one hour represent two labor-hours.

The supplied example models 10 biweekly clients at $150/visit (about $3,250/month), 10 turnovers at $170 ($1,700), and 3 deep/move cleans at $250 ($750), totaling about $5,700 monthly gross or roughly $68,000 annualized if sustained. Separately, 25 billable hours/week at $50/hour is about $5,400 monthly gross.

These are illustrations, not forecasts or profit estimates. They exclude costs including owner labor, driving, fuel, supplies, insurance, payment processing, advertising, equipment, cancellations, taxes, and administration.
