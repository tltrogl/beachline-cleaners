FAQ

Common questions.

The details people usually want to know before booking.

How is pricing determined?

We use the property size (square footage and bed/bath count), service type, current condition, frequency, and requested extras to determine the scope and quote. Deep cleans and move cleans may require photos or additional details for an accurate quote.

Do I need to be home?

No, you do not need to be home. Most of our clients provide us with a spare key or a door code so we can clean while they are out or at work. If you prefer to be home, that is completely fine too.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, we bring all of our own professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment. If you have specific products you prefer us to use for delicate surfaces, just let us know and leave them out for us.

What if I have pets?

Pets are welcome. Please secure any pet that is aggressive, highly anxious, or likely to interfere with cleaning so the visit can be completed safely.

What’s the difference between standard and deep cleaning?

Standard cleaning focuses on maintaining a space that is already in decent condition—wiping surfaces, vacuuming, mopping, and cleaning bathrooms. Deep cleaning addresses neglected areas, heavy buildup, detailed baseboard and trim cleaning, and intensive scrubbing.

Can you handle linens/restocking for rentals?

Linen resets and basic supply restocking are available by arrangement. We’ll confirm laundry access, storage locations, and the replenishment checklist before service begins.

What is your cancellation and access policy?

If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact us as soon as possible. Access instructions and any cancellation or lockout terms will be confirmed before the appointment is booked.

Request a quote online or call us to discuss your property, timing, and cleaning needs.
